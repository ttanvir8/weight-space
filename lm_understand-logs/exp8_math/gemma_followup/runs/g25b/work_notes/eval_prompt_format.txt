The evaluation prompt, exactly as the model sees it (thinking off), for a problem P:
PREFIX + P + SUFFIX, with
PREFIX = '<bos><start_of_turn>user\n'
SUFFIX = '\nPlease reason step by step, and put your final answer within \\boxed{}.<end_of_turn>\n<start_of_turn>model\n'
The model's answer follows SUFFIX directly. To score a text with a teacher-forced pass, send PREFIX + P + SUFFIX + answer as one raw text in "texts".
